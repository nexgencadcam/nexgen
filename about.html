About NEX GEN CAD CAM

Precision Digital Design for the Jewellery Industry

Bridging the gap between traditional craftsmanship and cutting-edge 3D technology to bring your jewellery visions to life.

Our Story

NEX GEN CAD CAM was founded with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between traditional jewellery craftsmanship and modern digital design technology. We recognized that jewellers needed reliable, high-precision CAD models that respect manufacturing constraints while pushing aesthetic boundaries.

Serving jewellers across Pakistan, we provide precise CAD/CAM solutions that transform concepts into production-ready reality. Our team combines technical expertise with a deep appreciation for the art of jewellery making, ensuring every design we create is as functional as it is beautiful.

Our Mission

To empower jewellers across Pakistan with world-class digital design services, making professional CAD/CAM accessible, accurate, and affordable. We strive to be the invisible technical partner behind your most successful pieces.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's leading jewellery CAD/CAM studio, recognized for precision, innovation, and unwavering commitment to our clients' success. We envision a future where every jeweller can leverage the power of digital design.

Phase 01

Consultation & Requirements

We discuss your vision, reviewing your sketches, reference imagery, gem profiles, and target dimensions to ensure casting viability and structural excellence.

  • Personalized design consultation
  • Manufacturing constraint analysis
Phase 02

High-Precision CAD Development

Our master 3D jewellery modelers build the design in MatrixGold and Rhino CAD with exact prong tolerances, pave settings, and hollowed-out weight optimizations.

  • Specialized MatrixGold jewellery modeling
  • Exact stone seats & metal distribution
Phase 03

3D Review & Collaborative Refinement

We provide ultra-realistic studio renders and multi-angle views for you and your clients. We implement modifications with fast turnaround until complete approval.

  • Photorealistic client presentation renders
  • Iterative adjustments with zero hassle
Phase 04

Production-Ready File Delivery

Delivery of error-free .STL, .3DM, or .STEP files ready for wax 3D printing, investment casting, or CNC milling with all technical specs documented.

  • Watertight STL ready for 3D printers
  • Full gem & metal weight reports
