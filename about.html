Our Story

NEX GEN CAD CAM was founded with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between traditional jewellery craftsmanship and modern digital design technology. We recognized that jewellers needed reliable, high-precision CAD models that respect manufacturing constraints while pushing aesthetic boundaries.

Serving jewellers across Pakistan, we provide precise CAD/CAM solutions that transform concepts into production-ready reality. Our team combines technical expertise with a deep appreciation for the art of jewellery making, ensuring every design we create is as functional as it is beautiful.

Our Mission

To empower jewellers across Pakistan with world-class digital design services, making professional CAD/CAM accessible, accurate, and affordable. We strive to be the invisible technical partner behind your most successful pieces.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's leading jewellery CAD/CAM studio, recognized for precision, innovation, and unwavering commitment to our clients' success. We envision a future where every jeweller can leverage the power of digital design.
