Phase 01

Share Your Vision & Requirements

Send us your design concept through a rough hand sketch, reference photo, or detailed description. Include ring size, gemstone dimensions, and preferred metal weight.

  • Technical feasibility assessment
  • Casting shrinkage & thickness validation
Phase 02

Precision 3D CAD Modelling

Our CAD specialists build the complete piece inside MatrixGold and Rhino CAD. Every prong, micro-pavé seat, hollow channel, and hinge is engineered with manufacturing tolerances.

  • Accurate stone seats & prong geometry
  • Precise metal weight calculation
Phase 03

Photorealistic Review & Client Revisions

You receive high-resolution 3D renders from multiple angles (and interactive previews) to share with your clients for approval. We tweak dimensions and styling until 100% perfection.

  • Multi-angle studio lighting renders
  • Fast revisions & fine-tuning
Phase 04

Production-Ready CAM Delivery

Once approved, we deliver clean, watertight .STL, .3DM, and .STEP files ready for 3D wax/resin printing, direct investment casting, or precision CNC milling.

  • Manifold, watertight STL meshes
  • Compatible with all major casting systems
