Section 01

Overview & Scope

Welcome to NEX GEN CAD CAM (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We provide professional jewellery CAD/CAM design, 3D modelling, digital prototyping, and manufacturing preparation services for retail jewellers, manufacturers, and bespoke designers across Pakistan and internationally.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, store, and protect your personal information and commercial design assets when you access our official website (https://nexgencadcam.com/), communicate with our design team, or submit jewellery project briefs.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to evaluate, quote, design, and deliver your jewellery CAD projects:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number (or WhatsApp contact), and your jewellery business or brand name.
  • Project Inquiries & Design Briefs: Information provided through our contact forms, such as requested jewellery categories (rings, pendants, earrings, bracelets, bridal sets), desired precious metal weights, diamond/gemstone dimensions, prong styles, and custom specifications.
  • Reference Files: Sketches, reference photographs, 2D vector artwork, gemstone maps, or preliminary 3D files that you upload or transmit to us for modeling.

B. Technical & Device Information Collected Automatically

  • Server Log Information: Browser type and version, operating system, IP address, referring URL, time stamps, and pages accessed. This technical data is used exclusively for site reliability, security monitoring, and anti-abuse verification.
Strict NDA Commitment

Client CAD Designs, 3D Models & IP Confidentiality

In the bespoke jewellery industry, design exclusivity and copyright protection are paramount. We treat every client’s jewellery concept as strictly confidential proprietary intellectual property.

1. Non-Disclosure & Zero Unauthorized Sharing

Your sketches, stone settings, dimension charts, master CAD files (.3dm, .stl, .step, .obj), and render proofs are held under a strict duty of non-disclosure. We guarantee that your custom designs will never be shared, licensed, or made available to any other client, competitor, or third party.

2. 100% Exclusive Client Ownership

Upon full settlement of our design service invoices, all completed production CAD files and deliverables become the 100% exclusive intellectual property of the client. NEX GEN CAD CAM retains no residual ownership rights over bespoke custom models commissioned by you.

3. No Portfolio Display Without Explicit Written Consent

We respect client privacy. We will never publish your proprietary CAD renders, video previews, or final models on our website portfolio, social media channels (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn), or promotional materials without obtaining your prior written approval.

4. Dedicated CAD Storage & Access Controls

All active and archived CAD files are stored on isolated, encrypted storage units accessible exclusively by assigned senior CAD engineers under non-disclosure obligations.

Section 04

How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and technical purposes, including:

  • Preparing accurate cost estimations, turnaround schedules, and feasibility assessments for your jewellery pieces.
  • Developing high-precision 3D CAD files tailored to your casting, stone-setting, and CNC/3D-printing tolerances.
  • Sharing 360-degree render previews and interactive design reviews for your revision and milestone sign-off.
  • Delivering final production-ready STL / 3DM files and providing post-delivery technical support.
  • Responding to your customer service inquiries via email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Ensuring network security, preventing spam form submissions, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or design assets to third parties or marketing brokers.

Section 05

Cookies & Local Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes minimal, privacy-conscious cookies and local browser storage to guarantee website stability and performance:

  • Essential Functional Cookies: Temporary session cookies that maintain user navigation states and secure form submissions.
  • UI State & Preferences: Local storage flags to remember visual display preferences, such as reduced motion settings or particle canvas rendering states.

We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising cookies or behavioral ad tracking pixels. You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies; however, some site visual effects or form validations may not function as intended without essential cookies enabled.

Section 06

Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with reputable infrastructure providers to operate our digital presence:

  • Formspree (Form Transmission): Our contact inquiries are securely processed through Formspree to route project briefs directly to our engineering mailbox via TLS-encrypted connections.
  • Google Fonts: Web fonts (Playfair Display & Inter) are served to optimize page styling and legibility across all devices.
  • Hosting & Content Delivery: Website assets are hosted on secure global content delivery networks with automated SSL/TLS certificate renewal.
  • Social Media Links: Outbound links to our verified profiles on Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Interacting with these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies.
Section 07

Data Security Safeguards

We deploy robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information:

  • 256-Bit SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between your web browser and our website is strictly encrypted over HTTPS with modern cipher suites.
  • Permanent HTTPS & HSTS: We enforce HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) and permanent 301 redirection so all traffic is channeled over encrypted protocols.
  • Restricted Internal Access: Client files, phone contacts, and design models are accessible solely by authorized personnel who require access to execute your project.
Section 08

Data Retention & Permanent Purge Rights

We retain client contact records and project files for the duration required to complete the commissioned CAD services and provide reasonable post-delivery warranty or resizing revisions.

Right to Immediate Purge: At any time following project delivery and settlement, you may request the complete and permanent deletion of your CAD files, sketches, and contact records from our active servers by emailing info@nexgencadcam.com. Upon receipt, we will securely wipe all local working copies within 10 business days.

Section 09

Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable international and national data protection principles, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a summary of the personal data and project records we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete contact details.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request deletion of your personal records and archived design files, subject to statutory bookkeeping requirements.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Object to specific non-essential uses of your information.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw any previously granted consent (such as voluntary portfolio display permissions) at any time with immediate forward effect.
